Privacy Policy

Effective October 17, 2026

TerryTorry is a fitness game for iPhone made by an independent developer (“we”). This policy explains what the app collects, why, who can see it, and how to delete it. The short version: we never learn your name or email, your location is used only while you're running, nothing is sold or used for advertising, and you can delete everything from inside the app.

1. Your account

You sign in with Sign in with Apple. We request no scopes, so Apple does not share your name or email address with us. We receive only an anonymous identifier that Apple generates for this app. You then choose a username, which is the only name other players ever see.

3. Who can see your data

Only members of the leagues you've joined can see your username, level, cosmetics, territory outline and area, and markers. There are no public leaderboards, no profiles, no search, and no way for someone outside your leagues to find you. Leagues are joined by invite code only.

4. Service providers

Your data is stored and processed by Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore, Firebase Authentication, App Check and Crashlytics) on Google Cloud infrastructure, under Google's privacy terms. Sign-in is handled by Apple. We use no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs, and we do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone else.

5. Retention and deletion

Territories are cleared automatically at the end of each season. Everything else is kept until you delete your account. To delete it, open the settings menu on the map and choose Delete Account. This permanently removes your user record, username, territories, markers, league memberships and progress, and revokes the app's Sign in with Apple authorisation. Leagues you created pass to their next member, or are deleted if you were the only one. Database backups are retained for up to 7 days for disaster recovery and are then gone.

6. Children

TerryTorry is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Security

Data travels over encrypted connections. Database access rules allow each player to change only their own data, and device attestation blocks requests that don't come from the genuine app. No system is perfectly secure, but we don't hold anything that identifies you beyond your chosen username.

8. Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the effective date above will move and the app will tell you on next launch.
