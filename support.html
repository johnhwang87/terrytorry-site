How does claiming land work?

Start a run, head out, and come back to where you started. When you return to the start, your route becomes a closed loop and everything inside it is your territory. A straight there-and-back line encloses nothing, so loop around instead of retracing your steps. Your newest saved loop replaces your old territory, like a high score.

It said “Loop Not Closed”

You need to get back to roughly your starting point, and the run has to be long enough to count (about 500 feet of recorded distance). GPS under trees or between tall buildings can drift, so give the app a moment at the start before you set off, and finish where you began. The run screen shows your live distance to the start once the loop is armed, and tells you when you're close enough to end.

Leagues and invite codes

Every league has an invite code like TERRA-NAME-XXXX, shown on the league's detail screen. Share it with friends; they enter it under Join League. Leagues hold up to 8 people. Only your league-mates can see your territory.

My territory disappeared

Seasons are weekly by default. When a season ends, every territory in the league is cleared, the biggest one is crowned champion, and everyone starts fresh. Your XP and level carry over. If Land Eater Mode is on, a league-mate can also swallow your territory by running a bigger loop that fully encloses it. You'll see a marker where it used to be.

Location and motion permissions

TerryTorry needs precise location while a run is in progress, including when the screen is off, so it can follow your route. It uses the step counter to sanity-check the distance. Neither is used when you're not running. Both can be changed any time in Settings → Privacy & Security.

Deleting your account

Open the settings menu on the map (the gear button) and choose Delete Account. Apple will ask you to confirm, then your username, territories, league memberships and progress are permanently removed and your Sign in with Apple connection is revoked. This can't be undone.
